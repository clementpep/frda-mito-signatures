## Units of independence
- Independent unit: cell line. Only two exist (one FRDA, one control)
- n per group (independent units, not samples): 1 FRDA line, 1 control line, in every condition
- Replicate structure: 3 culture replicates per condition (GEO: "Three cell culture replicates of each condition"); they are NOT independent donors. Any FRDA-vs-control contrast has n = 1 per group and is exploratory by construction. Treatment contrasts within GM04078 are within-line comparisons only

This is an inference from naming, flagged in each record: the "0 mismatches" result confirms treatment only, not the FRDA/control line assignment.
- Age, sex, passage, batch: **not provided** (GEO gives none for these lines; Coriell catalogue data are outside the allowlist and were not used).

## Known confounders and how they are handled
- Genotype is fully confounded with donor background (one line each): cannot be separated.
- DMSO vehicle: use DMSO columns as the reference for compound contrasts, not untreated.
- Culture batch / passage: not provided.
- Dose concentrations in uM are taken from GEO; checking them against the paper is still open (T-003).

## What this dataset can and cannot support
- Supports: within-line (GM04078) treatment contrasts with 3 culture replicates; reproduction of the paper's FXN effect of A-196.
- Does not support: any FRDA-vs-control inference about patients (n = 1 vs 1), or any inference that a compound effect in cultured cells has clinical meaning.
