## Units of independence
- Independent unit: cell line. Only two exist (one FRDA, one control)
- n per group (independent units, not samples): 1 FRDA line, 1 control line, in every condition
- Replicate structure: 3 culture replicates per condition (GEO: "Three cell culture replicates of each condition"); they are NOT independent donors. Any FRDA-vs-control contrast has n = 1 per group and is exploratory by construction. Treatment contrasts within GM04078 are within-line comparisons only

## Known confounders and how they are handled
- Genotype is fully confounded with donor background (one line each): cannot be separated.
- DMSO vehicle: use DMSO columns as the reference for compound contrasts, not untreated.
- Culture batch / passage: not provided.
- Dose concentrations in uM are taken from GEO; checking them against the paper is still open (T-003).

## QC (T-002)
Source: `results/P-003-qc/` (pipeline P-003, `analysis_kind=qc`). Same rules, stated in the run plan before the run as GSE104288 (genes with median >= 10: 13,877 of 14,177; outlier threshold Q1 - 3*IQR = 0.9293).

- **Outliers: 1 of 24 flagged: GM40unt1** (note: the outlier score is pooled over both lines, so for a control sample 18 of its 23 comparators are the other line; a within-line score is a deviation to report, not a rule change) (control_untreated, median correlation to all others 0.9276, versus 0.9633 for its two sister replicates). GM40dmso1 is just above the threshold (0.9297) with the same pattern (replicate 1 of the control line lower than replicates 2-3 in both conditions; all FRDA-line samples have median correlation >= 0.975). **Decision: retained** (no silent removal; n = 3 replicates per condition leave no margin to drop one). Any control-line contrast must be repeated without GM40unt1, and without GM40dmso1, as sensitivity analyses. The pooled score distribution is bimodal by line (control ~0.96, FRDA ~0.985), so the threshold is reachable only by control samples and the lower replicate-1 correlation reported as a possible culture/library effect (cause unknown, not documented in GEO).
- **Sex (inferred)**: all 24 columns have Y-score 9.6-10.0, i.e. male for both lines (no discordance between lines, none between replicates). XIST is **absent** from this matrix (14,177 genes), so the female marker could not be checked; 24/24 male with no female marker is a weak check. The Ensembl ids of the five Y genes were checked by the verifier on 2026-11-09 through NCBI Gene (all on chromosome Y; source NCBI Gene, not an Ensembl GRCh38 v98 lookup). No declared sex exists in GEO.
- **Structure (descriptive)**: PC1 (43.9% of variance) is read as separation of the two cell lines (genotype is fully confounded with line, 1 vs 1 independent unit); no p-values are reported for this series because the 3 columns per condition are culture replicates, not independent units. PC4 (5.8%) was not interpreted against condition. No batch variable in GEO.
- Decision per flagged sample: GM40unt1 flagged, retained, sensitivity analysis required downstream.

## What this dataset can and cannot support
- Supports: within-line (GM04078) treatment contrasts with 3 culture replicates; reproduction of the paper's FXN effect of A-196.
- Does not support: any FRDA-vs-control inference about patients (n = 1 vs 1), or any inference that a compound effect in cultured cells has clinical meaning.
