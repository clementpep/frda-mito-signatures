## Known confounders and how they are handled
- Age: mean difference +6.2 y (FRDA older); no significant difference at n = 18 vs 17, which is not evidence of balance. Include age as a covariate or run a sensitivity analysis in any downstream model.
- Sex: unknown from GEO; see above.
- Sensitivity: the control mean and SD depend on the 1-day-old line (C21808); T-002 / downstream models should repeat age adjustment without it. The value is reported verbatim from GEO and not corrected.
- Passage, culture and sequencing batch: not provided, cannot be adjusted for; state as a limitation.
- GAA repeat length / disease duration / treatment status of donors: not provided.

## QC (T-002)
Source: `results/P-003-qc/` (pipeline P-003, `analysis_kind=qc`). Rules stated in the run plan (autonomy/runs/2026-11-09-worker-T-002.md) before the pipeline was run (same author, no earlier commit): log2(x+1); genes with median >= 10 (13,983 of 16,531); per-sample score = median Spearman correlation to the 34 others; outlier if < Q1 - 3*IQR (threshold 0.8946); sex inferred from Y-score (mean log2(x+1) of RPS4Y1, DDX3Y, KDM5D, UTY, EIF1AY): male >= 3, female < 1, otherwise ambiguous. Nothing is removed.

- **Outliers: 0 of 35 flagged** (scores 0.923-0.962, Q1 0.939, Q3 0.953). Decision: all 35 retained. The neonatal control C21808 is not an outlier (0.9425); the age sensitivity noted above remains a downstream task.
- **Sex (inferred from expression, not declared by GEO)**: 18 male, 15 female, 2 ambiguous. By group: FRDA 11 male / 6 female / 1 ambiguous; control 7 / 9 / 1. Ambiguous: C8399 (Y-score 1.35, XIST 9.09) and F4230 (1.37, XIST 9.92): high XIST with weak Y signal, so female-like by XIST, but they do not meet the pre-set rule and are **left unassigned** (no post-hoc rule change; possible loss of Y signal or mosaicism not excluded). Discordances against declared sex: none can be listed, because GEO declares no sex. The titles C_UF / C_UM are a non-GEO suffix (hypothesis only): C_UF is inferred female (Y 0.21) and C_UM male (Y 8.43), i.e. consistent with the suffix. Sex is unbalanced between groups (male share 65% (11/17) FRDA vs 44% (7/16) control among assigned samples, descriptive only).
- **Structure (descriptive; 5 PCs x group / inferred sex / age = 15 uncorrected tests, Bonferroni threshold 0.0033 (post hoc: not in the pre-run rules))**: PC1-PC3 (15.1 / 13.2 / 9.0% of variance) vs group: Kruskal p = 0.017, 0.0017, 0.0021 (only PC2 and PC3 pass the Bonferroni threshold); PC4 (6.4%) vs inferred sex p = 0.0006 (passes); PC3 vs inferred sex p = 0.020 (does not pass); no PC vs age at p < 0.09 (n = 35, not evidence of absence). These associations are not adjusted for age (FRDA older by 6 y) or sex (unbalanced), and cannot be separated from unknown batch. No batch or library-date variable exists in GEO, so batch structure cannot be tested; library sums span 2.05e7-3.22e7.
- Decision per flagged sample: none flagged; C8399 and F4230 kept, sex = unassigned. Sex is a covariate candidate for downstream models; the 2 unassigned samples must be handled explicitly there (sensitivity run without them), not imputed.

## What this dataset can and cannot support
- Supports: donor-level FRDA vs control comparisons of fibroblast expression (n = 18 vs 17), exploratory or, if preregistered, confirmatory.
- Does not support: count-based models as published (only DESeq-normalised, non-integer values), adjustment for sex/passage/batch, or any claim about neural tissue. Association with disease status only; no causal or clinical claim.
