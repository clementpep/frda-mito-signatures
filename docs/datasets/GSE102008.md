## Sample characteristics
All 13 characteristic keys, parsed **by key per sample**: `pidn`, `sample number`, `batch`, `site`, `rin`, `status`, `Sex` (capital S in GEO), `age`, and for patients only `onset`, `duration`, `gaa1`, `gaa2`, `functional stage`.

**Parsing trap.** The series matrix writes `!Sample_characteristics_ch1` lines compacted: when a sample lacks a field the following fields move up one line (`duration` appears on 3 different lines, `functional stage` on 3, `gaa1` and `gaa2` on 2; see `characteristics_line_layout`). Reading "line 9 = gaa2" would silently assign durations to GAA2. Any later code must parse by key, as P-002 does.

GEO gives **no unit** for any field (recorded in `units` of `GSE102008_samples.json`). The paper states "disease duration in years", and in all 407 patients with `age`, `onset` and `duration`, `duration = age − onset` exactly (407/407), so onset, age and duration share one unit; years is the paper's wording for duration, not a GEO statement. `gaa1`/`gaa2` are repeat counts (paper: "number of GAA repeats").

### Definitions (GEO and paper)
GEO gives **no definition** of `functional stage`, `onset`, `duration`, `gaa1` or `gaa2`. From the paper (Nachun et al. 2018, Methods/Results, Europe PMC PMC6097013):
- Functional disability stage (FDS): "a scale from 0–6 based upon their mobility, with 0 indicating no impairment and 6 complete disability" (score "developed for the Friedreich's ataxia rating scale (FARS)"). GEO values are in 1–6 with half steps; 0 does not occur.
- Duration: "the disease duration (the difference between age of onset and age at draw)", "disease duration in years".
- GAA1: the paper says "the shorter of the two GAA repeat expansions (GAA1)", which implies GAA2 is the longer one; GAA2 is not otherwise defined in the paper text searched (keyword search of the full text for GAA1 / repeat); in GEO GAA1 is the larger value in 356/357 patients with both, which contradicts "shorter" as a reading of the label — **unresolved, do not assume which allele is which**.
- Paper: "Eighteen patients were compound heterozygotes with one repeat expansion and one loss-of-function point mutation in FXN"; GEO does not flag them.
- Phenotype subset in the paper: "Several phenotypic measures were available in a large subset of the FRDA patients (n = 308)"; compound heterozygotes were excluded from that analysis. GEO has no field flagging compound heterozygotes.

## Relatedness
GEO has **no family identifier**. Paper: "Most carriers were parents of patients, who are obligate carriers. Control subjects consisted of individuals known not to have any relatives with FRDA." Only 3 GSM carry a relative marker in `pidn`/title: `4044_mo` (GSM2721228), `4044_fa` (GSM2721229) (both Carrier; pidn 4044 is a Patient in the cohort) and `184_mom` (GSM2721230, Carrier; pidn 184 is **not** in the cohort); plus `pidn` = `LM` (GSM2721220, Carrier, no identifiable family). The other 224 carriers' links to patients are not identifiable. The "independent individuals" assumption is assumed, not shown, for statistics at the level of individuals, but patient–carrier pairs (parent–child) cannot be excluded or modelled, and the age difference between carriers (49.6) and patients (29.2) is consistent with, but not proof of, a parent–child structure.

That number matches the statement, but GEO does not say these are the 4; **treat as a probable, not a certain identification**. Only one array per `pidn` is in the 733, so the other array for these pidn is not in the 733 and no pair can be compared (sample numbers 2 (23 samples) and 3 (3 samples) also occur; GEO does not explain them).

Paper: "we checked blood frataxin levels in 95 enrolled controls and excluded 2 subjects with frataxin levels lower than the range observed in homozygote expansion carriers, leaving 93 controls for further analyses." Also, from the Methods: "A total of 17 outliers were removed from the full dataset and 6 outliers were removed the patient-only dataset".
Status of the reconciliation: **not reconcilable from GEO and the paper alone.** GEO has 94 controls, one more than the paper's 93, and no frataxin value, so the one control that should differ cannot be identified; 7 patients of the paper are not in GEO and nothing in the paper or GEO says which, or why (GEO states that outliers were removed in its processing, the paper reports 17 outliers removed from the full dataset; neither source ties those numbers to the 6-sample difference). Consequence for reproduction: a reproduction on GEO cannot be on the paper's exact cohort (418/228/93) and cannot apply its frataxin exclusion; counts of significant genes will differ for this reason in addition to method differences, and the comparison must say so (carry to T-028).

## Known confounders and how later analyses must handle them
- **Site**: controls are all CHOP. Include site as a covariate/stratum; a site-stratified or CHOP-only sensitivity analysis is needed for any control contrast.
- **Batch** (19 levels, strongly unbalanced by status): the matrix is said to be ComBat-corrected already. Do not correct twice without stating it; check batch structure in a PCA (T-028) and run a sensitivity analysis with batch as a covariate or random effect.
- **Age**: patients 29.2 vs carriers 49.6 vs controls 38.6 years, large differences (patient median 26, carrier median 50, control median 37.5); the paper itself writes age "was found to be collinear with all three [phenotypic] measures". Adjust for age in all status contrasts (this departs from the paper's differential-expression model, which removed age and sex for collinearity and kept only status and RIN; T-028 must quote and compare); add an age-matched sensitivity analysis; for patient-only severity analyses (FDS, GAA1, duration) age is collinear with duration (duration = age − onset exactly), so duration cannot be added to a model that already has age and onset.
- **Sex**: carriers are 61.8% female vs 42.6% controls and 46.7% patients; adjust, and run a sex-stratified check.
- **RIN**: similar medians across statuses (8.6 / 8.5 / 8.7) but with outliers down to 1.0; use as covariate, as the paper did ("only disease status and RIN were available to use as variables").
- **Relatedness**: not modelled (see above); state as a limitation.
- **Missingness**: severity fields are missing completely outside patients and partly inside them (FDS 334/411 = 81.3%, GAA1 376/411 = 91.5%, GAA2 357/411 = 86.9%, onset/duration 407/411 = 99.0%); analyses use complete cases per field, with n stated, never imputation.
- **Cell composition**: whole blood, no cell counts in GEO: differences may reflect cell-type proportions; not adjustable from GEO.

## What this dataset can and cannot support
- Supports: individual-level patient vs carrier vs control comparisons in whole blood at n = 411 / 228 / 94, and patient-only associations with severity fields at the n above; exploratory or, if preregistered, confirmatory.
- Does not support: the paper's exact cohort or frataxin exclusion, raw-intensity re-processing without the non-normalised file, family-aware models, adjustment for cell composition, any claim about neural tissue. Association with status/severity only; no causal or clinical claim.
