## Context
Owner request (interactive session, 2026-10-17): the work must be useful to the FRDA community by
properly analysing data obtained from patients, not by stacking theoretical hypotheses on cell
models. The seed datasets are fibroblast lines (GSE104288: 18 + 17 donors; GSE145115: one line per
group), far from the patients' clinical picture.

A live GEO E-utilities probe on 2026-10-17 (interactive session; not saved as an artifact — T-026
and T-008 re-derive and save it) found public, non-DUA patient data with clinical annotations,
notably **GSE102008**: 733 whole-blood samples on Illumina HT-12 (GPL10558) — in GEO, 411
patients, 228 heterozygous carriers, 94 controls, one sample per individual, two sites (CHOP 568,
UCLA 165) — with sex, age, age at onset, disease duration, GAA repeat lengths (GAA1 for 376
patients) and functional stage (334 patients).

Its source paper is Nachun et al. 2018 (PMID 29790959, Hum Mol Genet; full text PMC6097013, read
through Europe PMC on 2026-10-17). What the paper itself reports, not the GEO summary:
- 739 subjects enrolled at UCLA and CHOP — patients n = 418, carriers n = 228, controls n = 93
  (Table 1; 2 of 95 controls excluded for blood frataxin below the carrier range). These differ
  from the GEO counts (411 / 228 / 94): T-026 and T-028 must reconcile or report the gap; the
  frataxin exclusion cannot be applied from GEO.
- "829 transcripts were DE between patients and controls (global FDR = 0.012), 1078 between
  patients and carriers (global FDR = 0.0017) and 182 between carriers and controls (global
  FDR = 0.018)".
- Within patients, "1508 transcripts significantly associated with FDS" (functional disability
  stage, FARS scale 0–6), "280 transcripts significantly associated with GAA1" and "13
  transcripts significantly associated with disease duration"; age, collinear with these
  measures, was regressed out first.
- **Mitochondrial modules associated with disability are already reported.** In the
  patients-only FDS co-expression network, "the yellow module (coeff. = −0.0093, log BF = 3.01,
  pp = 1.0) and the red module (coeff. = −0.0077, log BF = 1.91, pp = 1.0)" are associated with
  FDS (global FDR = 1.0 × 10⁻⁴); the yellow module is enriched for "the mitochondrial
  respiratory chain complex (20 genes, log BF = 1.89)" and the red one for "mitochondrial
  translation (27 genes, log BF = 4.44)". Lower mitochondrial-module expression with higher
  disability in patients' blood is therefore prior published evidence, not a new finding.
- Other analyses in the paper: neutrophil degranulation is the top enrichment of up-regulated
  genes; cell-type deconvolution found only natural killer cell proportions differing by status;
  ComBat batch correction was used for the networks.
- The "27-gene expression panel" linked to inflammation, lipid metabolism and apoptosis appears in
  the GEO series summary, not in the paper; it is not used as a reproduction target.

What remains untested, hence the room for a contribution (narrow, and to be confirmed by the
novelty checks): (1) **independent replication** of the mitochondrial–disability association in
other patient cohorts and tissues — the paper's modules are data-driven and come from one cohort;
(2) a priori mitochondrial gene-set scores (fixed before looking) instead of data-driven modules;
(3) the status trend controls / carriers / patients for these scores; (4) their association with
GAA1, age at onset and disease duration (the paper's GAA1 and duration analyses were
transcript-wise, not on mitochondrial sets); (5) robustness to blood cell-type composition.

Other patient-derived series to assess: GSE30933 (PBMCs), GSE243874 (PBMCs by onset group),
GSE226646 (skeletal muscle biopsies), GSE105052 (plasma miRNA with cardiopathy, diabetes,
neuropathy), GSE200907.

## Decision (owner's call)
Decided by the owner in the interactive session of 2026-10-17 ("il faut que ce travail ait du
sens, donc analyser proprement des examens faits sur des patients pour aider la commu
scientifique ! Pas juste faire des hypothèses théoriques"); this session wrote the record.

1. **Primary evidence comes from patients.** The main question becomes: does the published
   association between lower mitochondrial expression and higher disability in patients' blood
   hold with a priori mitochondrial gene sets, across the status groups (controls, carriers,
   patients — a status trend, since frataxin level and not the group is the actual dose), with
   GAA1, age at onset and disease duration, after adjusting for age, sex, site, batch, RNA
   quality and blood cell composition, and **in independent patient cohorts**?
   Association only, never causation or clinical benefit. Any "new" claim needs a novelty check
   against Nachun et al. 2018 (its mitochondrial modules included).
2. Replication in independent patient cohorts (other blood series, muscle) comes before any cell
   model; a split of GSE102008 (e.g. by site) is internal validation, not independent replication; fibroblasts (GSE104288) and the A-196 intervention (GSE145115) become supporting,
   cross-tissue and mechanistic evidence (M4 stays, lower priority).
3. Unchanged rules: public non-DUA data only, reproduce the published GSE102008 result before any
   new claim, preregister confirmatory analyses, statistics on individuals.
4. Individual-level clinical registries (FACOMS, EFACTS: examinations, scales, imaging) are under
   data-access agreements and stay out of scope; a partnership is the owner's decision.
