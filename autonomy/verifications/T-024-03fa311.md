## Findings
### Blocking
1. Launch item, "chez 18 personnes malades et 17 personnes non malades" follows "données publiques" and reads as the whole
   project. 18/17 is GSE104288 only; GSE145115 has 1 FRDA line and 1 control line (docs/datasets/GSE145115.md:12-14), exploratory.
   Fixed = name both studies, say the 18/17 are donors of skin cell lines from one study, and that the second uses one line per
   group so cannot support conclusions on patients.
2. "chaque échantillon … relié à sa description officielle, sans erreur": what T-001 checked is column-to-GSM matching (35/35,
   24/24) and genotype/treatment agreement; GSE145115's FRDA/control line is inferred from the title prefix
   (GSE145115.md:33), and QC (T-002) is "Not yet done". Fixed = say what was matched, and that quality control is not done.
3. "Le projet ne cherche pas de traitement": the plan (M4/M5, T-013 to T-016, backlog:33) ranks public perturbation signatures as
   preclinical hypotheses and tests A-196 on the signature. Also the section "Ce que ça ne veut pas dire" omits the cell-model limit
   and the sample size required by STYLE.md rule 4. Fixed = e.g. "ne produit pas de traitement et ne teste rien sur des malades;
   à terme peut classer des pistes à examiner en laboratoire"; add that skin cells are not nerve tissue and that samples are small.
4. src/frda/news.py `problems()`: the evidence ceiling and claim requirement run only for kind=finding. A milestone or weekly
   item can print "Niveau de preuve : confirmé par une analyse annoncée à l'avance" (EVIDENCE_PLAIN) with no claim behind it, in Telegram
   and the hub. Fixed = apply the ceiling whenever `evidence` is set (claims required), or forbid `evidence` on non-finding kinds;
   add a test for a milestone item and one with two claims of different levels.
5. src/frda/news.py FORBIDDEN_FR: not matched (I ran them) guérira, guérissent, remèdes, prouvent, prouvera, percées; auditor also
   found causent/causé, révolutionne, "espoir pour les personnes malades". The gate's stated purpose is to stop overselling before
   it goes to outsiders. Fixed = stems with optional suffixes, a parametrised per-form regression test; keep the negation case
   ("ne prouve pas") usable in the "ne veut pas dire" section.
