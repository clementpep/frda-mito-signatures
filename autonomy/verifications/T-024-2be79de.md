## Findings
### Blocking
1. src/frda/news.py `_negated` / NEGATION_RE / NEGATION_WINDOW: a negation word (`pas`, `sans`, `ni`, `no`, `ne`) within 30 chars of a banned word
   lets it through in the limits section, even when the banned word is affirmed ("Pas de preuve mais cela prouve", "Il n'y a pas de doute que ça guérit").
   The clause split ignores "mais", "et", "car", "que", "donc", "but". Telegram and hub would publish an affirmative claim.
   Fixed = allow only explicit grammatical negations of the banned word (ne/n' + up to 2 words + word + pas/jamais/plus; "aucun(e)/pas de/pas un(e) + word";
   English "not/no ... cure") or drop the exception and rewrite limits text without banned words; add the failing inputs above as tests; also accept the
   typographic apostrophe ’ in n’.
2. src/frda/news.py `wording_problems` / FORBIDDEN_FR: (a) no Unicode NFC normalisation, so a decomposed "guérit" bypasses every accented stem (also claims.check_claim: out of
   scope here, mention in the report); (b) therapeutic and demonstration wording is not covered: thérapie / thérapeutique, médicament, démontre, établit,
   "responsable de", "entraîne", "validé/répliqué" in prose, English therapy and breakthrough. CLAUDE.md rule 4 forbids therapeutic wording in claims; the news
   gate exists to enforce the same for outsiders. Fixed = NFC-normalise title and body before matching, add the stems above (keep them negatable only per B1),
   parametrised tests for each.
