## Findings
### Blocking
1. RD-002 (Context, last paragraph before "Other patient-derived series", line ~38-40) says the paper does
   not "test ... association with clinical measures within patients" for mitochondrial gene sets, and
   cites the two mitochondrial modules without naming their network. In the paper both modules belong to the
   patients-only FDS co-expression network: yellow (respiratory chain complex, 20 genes, log BF 1.89;
   FDS coeff. -0.0093, log BF 3.01) and red (mitochondrial translation, 27 genes, log BF 4.44; FDS
   coeff. -0.0077, log BF 1.91), global FDR 1.0e-4. The paper also regresses transcripts on GAA1 (280)
   and duration (13). A mitochondrial module associated with a clinical measure in patients is therefore
   already published, and the "room for a contribution" is overstated. Fixed looks like: (a) RD-002 states
   the FDS-network location and these coefficients/log BF, quoted from the paper; (b) the contribution is
   narrowed to what is untested (a priori gene-set scores, a status trend, GAA1/onset/duration, independent
   cohorts, tissue); (c) matching wording in T-029/T-030/PLAN/brief where it implies the clinical association is new;
   (d) T-027 requires the FDS-network module targets (coefficient and log BF), not only the enrichment.
