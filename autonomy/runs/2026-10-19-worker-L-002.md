## lit-verify (full corpus, 1277 ids, online)
```
- MED:40919203: recorded title differs from Europe PMC (0.85)
- MED:32249386: recorded title differs from Europe PMC (0.89)
- MED:42383006: recorded title differs from Europe PMC (0.87)
lit verify: FAIL
```
Cause (checked against the REST API): Europe PMC returns titles with escaped markup
(`&lt;i&gt;frdA&lt;/i&gt;`, `Na&lt;sup&gt;+&lt;/sup&gt;`); `lit-add` stores them unescaped (`<i>frdA</i>`) and the
similarity check in `src/frda/literature.py` (threshold 0.9) compares the two forms. The metadata
is right (ids, titles, DOIs resolve); the tool's comparison is too literal. Not worked around: I did not
hand-edit corpus titles (P5.4) nor touch `src/` or the threshold (P5.5; not an infra task). Proposed
infra task: normalise markup/entities on both sides in the comparison. MED:40919203 is a
*Salmonella* `frdA` (fumarate reductase) paper, an expected E1 exclusion at L-003.
